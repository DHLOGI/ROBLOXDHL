About Us

Our Mission

We aim to provide the most realistic, professional, and exciting cargo roleplay experience within the Roblox aviation community.

Global Operations

Connecting players across different virtual continents with daily flights, precise logistics, and a highly active community.

Our Fleet

Boeing 757-200F

The backbone of our heavy cargo operations. Capable of carrying large payloads across long distances with excellent efficiency.

Delivery Vans

Available Careers

Flight Dispatcher

Control the skies. Assign routes, monitor weather conditions, and ensure the safety of all our virtual flights.

Cargo Pilot

Take control of our heavy aircraft. Fly worldwide routes and deliver critical supplies on time, every time.

Ground Crew

Load and unload cargo efficiently. Drive our iconic yellow trucks and keep the logistics chain moving flawlessly.
